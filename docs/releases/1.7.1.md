nspawn 1.7.1 fixes a copy that failed now and then on a busy host.

## Fixed

- **`cp` failed with "Try again".** `cp` looks paths up inside the machine
  with openat2 and RESOLVE_IN_ROOT, and the kernel refuses such a lookup
  when a `..` on the way crosses a rename or a mount anywhere on the host,
  since it can no longer vouch that the lookup stayed below the root; its
  manual leaves the retry to the caller. A path or a link with `..` (a
  relative link such as `/etc/localtime -> ../usr/share/zoneinfo/...`) could
  then fail the whole copy while the host renamed files elsewhere. The lookup
  is tried again, up to 32 times.

## Requirements

As for 1.7.0.
