nspawn 2.3.2 keeps needrestart from restarting the machines and the units nspawn runs
for them after an apt upgrade.

## Fixed

- **needrestart leaves the machines and nspawn's units alone.** The deb installs
  `/etc/needrestart/conf.d/nspawn.conf`. After an upgrade of nspawn, needrestart
  restarted each machine's health runner, port proxy and Docker API service: the health
  went back to `starting` and IPv6 connections through the proxy were cut. After an
  upgrade of systemd-nspawn, needrestart 3.6 (Ubuntu 24.04) restarted the machines
  together with systemd-machined, and a machine could fail to come back with "Machine
  already exists". They take the new binaries at the machine's next start.

## Requirements

As for 2.0.0.
