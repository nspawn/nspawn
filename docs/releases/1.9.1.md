nspawn 1.9.1 keeps the flags out of TAB until a dash asks for them.

## Changed

- **TAB offers names first.** On an empty word, completion offers the names
  an argument takes and the subcommands alone: `nspawn stop <TAB>` lists the
  running machines and `nspawn <TAB>` the commands, without the flags mixed
  in. The flags come once the word starts with `-` (`-` brings the short
  forms, `--` the long ones), or where there is no name to offer, as after
  `stop` with nothing running. The values of an option, as after `--network`,
  are offered as before.

## Requirements

As for 1.9.0.
