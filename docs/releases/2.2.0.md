nspawn 2.2.0 fixes what moving a whole server of compose projects to nspawn brought up,
and what a review of compose and of the builder found. compose gains anonymous volumes,
networks made anew when their definition changes, and a `start` that brings up what a
service depends on; the builder unpacks archives with everything they hold and keeps
the times and file capabilities of the context.

## Fixed

- **Machines keep their network when ufw is enabled while they run.** ufw sets
  FORWARD's policy to DROP when it is enabled, and so does docker when it is the one
  that turns IP forwarding on. nspawn added its exception only where the policy dropped
  already when a machine started, so a machine started before lost its published IPv4
  ports and its way beyond the bridge until it started again. Where ufw or docker is
  installed, the exception now goes to FORWARD ahead of time, which neither empties.
- **An app's program no longer finds systemd-nspawn's notify socket.** systemd-nspawn
  gives its payload `NOTIFY_SOCKET=/run/host/notify`, which only root may write to:
  ejabberd logged `Cannot send READY=1 notification` and forgejo `failed to dial
  NOTIFY_SOCKET` when they ran as their own user. The variable is set empty for apps,
  which their clients take for none; booted machines keep it.
- **compose leaves a bind source it cannot look into to the service.** A source under
  a directory only root reads, such as `/etc/letsencrypt/live/NAME/fullchain.pem`, was
  taken for missing and made as the user running compose, which failed with
  `Permission denied`. The service, which runs as root, now mounts it.
- **`logs --since` and `--until` take durations and times.** `--since 5m` failed, as
  the text went to journalctl as it was. A duration ago (`5m`, `1h30m`), Unix seconds
  and RFC 3339 times and dates are read, and journalctl's own forms (`-1h`, `"10 min
  ago"`) still work. `events` takes the same.
- **`logs --tail`** is taken for `-n`, `all` included.
- **`nspawn images` lists the images** instead of printing its help; `images ls` stays.

## compose

- **`start` brings up what a service depends on** and waits for its conditions, up to
  `--wait-timeout`; a dependency that fails is said with `dependency failed to start`,
  one that exits with another code with `service "NAME" didn't complete successfully:
  exit N`. Without the files, what depends on what comes from the machines' labels.
- **`down SERVICE`** takes that service alone with the files, and, with `-p NAME` and
  no `-f`, which takes the project from the machines' labels, the services that depend
  on it as well. The project's networks are tried every time, and
  one a machine still uses stays with `Resource is still in use`. `stop`, `pause` and
  `restart` act on the project's services only; `kill` with nothing running says `No
  container to kill`.
- **Anonymous volumes.** A volume without a source, or a `VOLUME` of the image nothing
  mounts over, gets an nspawn volume named after the machine, `MACHINE_anon_HEX`. A
  machine made anew keeps its own, `up -V` makes new ones, `down -v` and `rm -v` remove
  them.
- **A network whose definition changed is made anew** on `up`, with the project's
  machines on it.
- **`up` attached** watches the services it names, else those it attaches to, runs the
  `pre_stop` hooks when it stops them, and exits with 130 on Ctrl-C.
- **`logs`**: `-f` follows machines that start after it and says when one exits, `-t`
  stamps each line with its time in the journal in RFC 3339 UTC, `--index` needs one
  service, and a duration of `--since` or `--until` takes no days or weeks. One-off and
  orphan machines are left out.
- **`exec`, `port` and `cp`** pick the replica `--index` names, else the lowest
  numbered one; `cp` into a service copies into each of its replicas. `run` refuses `--service-ports` with `--publish` and a
  tty it cannot have, takes `-e KEY` from the project's environment (`.env` included),
  and removes the anonymous volumes of a `--rm` machine.
- **`ls`** takes `--filter name=` and `--format` (table, json or a template); `ps --filter` says what it cannot take; `port` lists the ports a service
  publishes when the asked one is not among them.
- **The files**: `network_mode: host` leaves out the published ports with a note;
  secrets written in the file or taken from the environment get their `mode`, `uid` and
  `gid`; a variable set to null in `environment` is unset; the words after a
  `CMD-SHELL` script of a healthcheck are its arguments, and `start_interval` is
  checked.
- **Flags**: `down --volume`, `up --y`, `run --no-TTY`, `--labels` and `--volumes` are
  taken as their current names, the first two with a notice; sizes and filters are
  checked as they are read. `build --provenance=false` and `--sbom=false` are taken, and
  a `--secret` of a variable that is not set is an empty secret.

## Builds

- **ADD of a local archive** unpacks it into the destination: an entry replaces what is
  at its path unless both are directories, symlinks on the way are followed inside the
  destination, a parent the archive does not list is made 0755, and owners (unless
  `--chown`), modes, times (a directory's once its contents are written, a time of 0
  kept), FIFOs, device nodes and extended attributes are kept. A V7 tar and a zstd
  stream that starts with a skippable frame are archives too.
- **The context keeps its times and file capabilities**: directory times, times of 0
  and nanoseconds, and `security.capability`, which reaches the layer and the cache
  key. A context tar on standard input keeps its owners, FIFOs and device nodes.
- **ADD of a URL** gives the file its `Last-Modified` time, the epoch without one, and a
  new `Last-Modified` runs the step again.
- **`COPY --parents`** of a source its `--exclude` leaves out whole fails with
  `"/PATH": not found`.
- **A `RUN` step's sandbox**: the hostname is `buildkitsandbox`, a `--mount=type=tmpfs`
  is mode 1777, CAP_SYS_BOOT is dropped, `NOTIFY_SOCKET` is empty, and a step whose
  process a signal ends exits with 128 plus the signal's number. A `WORKDIR` or working
  directory with a file in its way fails with `not a directory`, and a working directory
  made for the step belongs to its user.
- **`SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH`**, as a build argument or a global `ARG` with a value, is the
  time of the image and of the history entries the build adds; a value that is not a
  number fails with `invalid SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH: VALUE`.
- **The cache key** leaves out the proxy variables, the addresses of `--add-host`, the
  id and sharing of cache mounts, and the instructions that only set the image's
  settings before a step, and takes `--exclude`, file capabilities and a URL's
  `Last-Modified`. The keys change with it: the first build after the upgrade runs its
  steps again.
- **Reading files of an image** (`/etc/passwd` and `/etc/group` for `USER` and
  `--chown`) opens only regular files: a device node or FIFO at those paths is refused
  instead of reaching the host's device.
- `# check=` and `BUILDKIT_DOCKERFILE_CHECK` are checked for their words, a missing
  secret says `secret ID: not found`, and `--iidfile` is removed before the build.
